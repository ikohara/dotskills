Observed by the `tanto-sweep-2` Kanri (`dotskills-a1`) at its exit,
2026-09-15: a gap in how a consolidated ruling generalizes across topics and
roles. `tanto-sweep-2`'s own R-4 recorded the Kikaku decision
`2026-09-14-shoroku-at-close`'s T2-consolidation policy for that topic's own
Keikaku exit; `tanto-project-config` carries an equivalent. When
`shoroku-at-close`'s own Sekkei reached its final boundary in that tenure, no
existing ruling covered a *Sekkei* exit or *that* topic's own sessions —
despite the decision's own wording being "every topic's shoroku human-check
consolidates to one... no exception to any of its 7 points." The Kanri had
to re-derive the extension (recorded as R-3 in `shoroku-at-close`'s own
ledger) rather than cite an existing one.

A candidate for the decision file itself, or for `roles/kikaku.md`'s handling
of a decision like this one: state explicitly that a consolidation ruling
covers every role's exit at every topic it names, not only the role and topic
the first Kanri happened to phrase it for — so a later Kanri does not need to
re-derive the same reasoning a third time for `tanto-project-config`'s own
Sekkei or Keikaku, if either reaches its final boundary before this design
lands.

Related: issue-d072 (a ruling's home and numbering — this issue is about its
*reach*, not its home), issue-b31c (two Kikaku decisions diverging on topic
order), decision-d538 (a Sekkei or Keikaku writes its exit proposal unasked at
its final boundary — the moment at which the consolidation ruling has to be
found or re-derived). The Kikaku decision lives under `.tanto/kikaku/`,
outside the six document types, so it is named here by title and date rather
than path-linked.
