Source: shoroku shoki-seat S-86

The `shoki-seat` fix wave's Task 14 carried "should read" text that Kanri
wrote into the batch prompt for `roles/kanri.md`. A reviewer proved two of its
phrases wrong against the tree — a reason that said a gone seat is "resumed"
where the Not listed bullet resumes only a seat with a transcript, and a
"remove it" whose object an aside had made ambiguous — and one line could not
pass the repository's markdownlint. The Jisso took one fix round under a
ruling, confined to the words the findings named, and listed each of the
three differences in its report with a needle to revert it, instead of
putting a Rulings needed to Kanri and waiting.

Neither the contract nor the Jisso role says which is right. The cost either
way is a rework batch — a new Jisso, a prompt, a boundary — against three
phrases the human never saw in Kanri's own words, in a file `AGENTS.md` says
only the human approves. A candidate rule: a verified reviewer finding on
mandated wording may take a fix round only when the change is confined to the
words the finding names and the report lists each difference with a revert
needle; anything wider goes to Rulings needed. Kin issue-cabf (the same
question for a mandated code block).
