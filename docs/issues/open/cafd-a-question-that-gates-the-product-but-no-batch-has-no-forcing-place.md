Source: shoroku tanto-bg-seats S-63

`bgIsolation` — ledger open question 1 — decides whether a background Kanri can
write the roster at all. It sat unanswered for seven batches. Every batch report
carried the line "nothing in the remaining batches is blocked on this answer",
which was true of the batches and false of the product.

Nothing in the protocol forces a question of that kind to the human before the
close. An open question that blocks no batch is, by every signal the batch loop
has, not urgent; the one place it belongs is the kessai's numbered list, and
Kanri's close procedure does not say so.

The remedy touches Kanri's close procedure — a step that sweeps the ledger's
open questions and carries any question gating the product into the kessai's
numbered list, distinct from the questions that gate a batch. That is a decision
about the procedure, not a one-sentence correction, which is why it is filed
here rather than fixed.

Related: issue-aa37, whose own closing condition is this very question.
