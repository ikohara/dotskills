A live-check implementation seat is long-lived by nature: many wake-ups over
several sittings, no subagent dispatched, ending far over its ceiling.
Rotating the seat by sitting, rather than by batch, is the cheaper shape.

A replacement trigger for a Jisso that the ceiling does not cover — by sitting
rather than by batch — is a decision beside issue-6620 (whether
`ceiling.jisso` earns its keep) and issue-c44b (a ceiling for the measuring
roles), neither of which names a live-check seat.

Related: issue-6620, issue-c44b.
