Source: shoroku run-owned-seats S-74

A plan that edits the tanto skill stops its Keikaku at the cold read and
forbids creating a role before its last batch. In the run-owned-seats run the
boundary verdicts of batches A to D raised eighteen plan-mandated Important
findings (four, three, three, and eight) whose corrections are text outside
the plan's passages, so each needed a plan amendment nobody could make. All
eighteen were carried to one fix wave, and the restarted spawner met the
acceptance scene with the ones in `spawner.js` still unfixed.

The contract has no route between "amend the plan" and "wait for the fix
wave" for such a plan — keeping Keikaku until the last batch lands is one
candidate. This refines issue-1c5a for the skill-editing case.
