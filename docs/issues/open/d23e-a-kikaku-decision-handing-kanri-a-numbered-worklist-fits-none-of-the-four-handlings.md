Source: inbox 2026-09-24-kikaku-decision-numbered-worklist-fifth-handling

`roles/kanri.md`'s `decision: <path>` paragraph, and its parallel in
`SKILL.md`, name four handlings of a Kikaku decision: a topic's spec-stage
`I-n`, the next topic's between-plans input, a stage's Check answer by
exception, and a plain `S-n` source row — anything else falls under the
source-row case by default.

A decision whose "What Kanri should do with it" section names several
distinct actions directly — a ruling to record, a Session events line, a
line queued for another seat, an `S-n` row, sometimes more than one of these
together — fits none of the four, and its section has to spell out each
action by hand. In the reporter's run this was a majority of one topic's
decisions (3 of 4 in one closing session, and a similar cluster on one
earlier date), so it recurs across a topic's life rather than once.

The reporter proposes a fifth handling: "Kikaku hands Kanri a numbered
worklist directly" — Kanri follows the decision file's own numbered list
rather than forcing it into the source-row case. Whether to add it is a
change to the decision-file protocol decision-9cc5 set, and so decision-9cc5's
to amend, not a sentence the recommender can write.
