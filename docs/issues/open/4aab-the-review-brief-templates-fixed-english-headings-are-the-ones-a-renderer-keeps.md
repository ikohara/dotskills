Source: shoroku experience-layer S-24

At the `experience-layer` spec stage (2026-09-30) the brief writer's first
render left three of the eight headings in English — `How to answer`,
section 5's, and the unsettled section's. The template's fixed English
headings are the ones a renderer is likeliest to keep; the form check's
"headings in the human's language" rule caught it, at the cost of one resume
(69 s). A `render this heading too` note beside each fixed heading in
`templates/review-brief.md` would spare the resume.

The same gap from another run is issue-fb90's finding 9: two prose phrases
of the template's last section ("no answer needed unless you object", "an
answer here decides …") stayed in English because the template does not
list them as form markers. The repair touches several non-adjacent headings
and the form rule together.
