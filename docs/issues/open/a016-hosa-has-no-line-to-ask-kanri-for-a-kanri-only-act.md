Source: shoroku shoki-seat S-50

Hosa, whose chores come from the human, sent Kanri a line shaped as a
`chore:` ("chore: clean up probe residue — …") when the work needed
Kanri-only acts (`claude rm`, a branch delete). The direction is inverted —
Kanri hands chores to Hosa — and the contract has no line for "Hosa asks
Kanri to act for the human". Kanri held it, put it to the human, and the
human did it.

A one-line route in `roles/hosa.md`, `needs-kanri: <what> — <why>`, answered
by Kanri's usual human-access question in `roles/kanri.md`, would make the
held case the written one. It spans two files and is a decision on the
contract's shape. Kin issue-3a7c (Hosa's slot protocol has no receiver).
