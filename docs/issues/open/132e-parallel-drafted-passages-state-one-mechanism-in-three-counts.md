Five passages of the run-owned-seats plan, written by different `plan.draft`
drafters, state what a failed wake is and what follows it in three counts:
it fails, it fails twice, and `unsent:` after a retry. No passage rewrote the
Replace table's first row, so the table the contract points to kept the old
"SendMessage errors, then resume" wording. The disagreement cost two
fix-wave rulings and a rework.

A mechanism restated in several parallel-drafted documents needs one passage
that owns its wording, with the others quoting it; the `plan.draft` brief
could say so.
