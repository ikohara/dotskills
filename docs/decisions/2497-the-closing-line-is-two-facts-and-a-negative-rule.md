## Context

issue-f293 recorded that a seat's turn-ending text was ungoverned and read as a
dependency — a window would say, in its own words, whether it thought it was
still needed. req-04f5 asks instead that a seat's last words name facts a human
or another seat can check against a file, and that a seat never offer its own
opinion of whether it is still needed.

## Options

- **Two facts and a negative rule**, written from an English form and rendered
  in the chat's language: where the work landed, which step of the contract
  still runs through the seat, and no statement of the seat's own necessity.
- **A `削除可: 今` line with a `失うもの: なし` clause** — the seat states that
  it can be disposed of now and what would be lost. Rejected: it is a seat's
  opinion of its own necessity, which R-6 of `shoroku-at-close` showed to be
  worth little.

## Decision

The closing line is two facts and a negative rule, rendered in the chat's
language from an English form (the closing-line decision file of 2026-09-17
§1).

## Consequences

- The line is checkable: both facts name a file or a contract step, so a reader
  verifies rather than trusts.
- The English form is the source and the chat language is the rendering, which
  is the same reader/input split the review brief already makes.
- issue-f293's cause is closed with the rejected form on record, because it is
  the kind of alternative a later designer proposes again.
